Maldives seaplane vs commercial airliners

How a Twin Otter seaplane compares with the airliners you fly

The Twin Otter on floats that carries visitors to Maldives resorts is small, slow and low, and it lands on water. Here it is next to five well-known aircraft: the ATR 72 turboprop, the Airbus A320neo, the Boeing 737-800, the Boeing 787-9 and the Airbus A350-900.

How high they fly

A seaplane on a resort transfer stays below about 5,000 ft. Its cabin is not pressurised, the trips are short, and the pilots fly by sight. Jets climb above Mount Everest, where thin air means less drag and lower fuel burn. Their cabins are pressurised: a 787's cabin is kept at the equivalent of about 6,000 ft.
